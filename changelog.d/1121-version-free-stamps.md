### Fixed
- **A toolkit upgrade no longer rewrites every tracked diagram.** Generated Mermaid diagrams
  (class, contract, Silver and Gold ERDs), `ubiquitous-language.ttl`, `concept-guide.md`, the
  bounded-context design notes and the per-domain DDD report used to carry the toolkit
  version. These files are drift-gated, so every upgrade changed all of them by one line and
  failed `pr-validate.yml` until someone ran `project --target erd --target ddd`. They now say
  only `Generated by kairos-ontology -- do not edit`; the hub's `uv.lock` already records
  which toolkit drew them. Expect one last regeneration on the upgrade to this release.
- **`update --upgrade` names the regeneration commands.** After a hub upgrade it prints
  `compile --all --emit --confirm-emit` and `project --target erd --target ddd`, the two
  outputs CI regenerates and diffs, so the upgrade PR is not red on its first push.
